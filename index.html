    Catalog audit in five passes

    Find what is blocking discovery, conversion or approval
    1. Identity. Brand, product IDs, category and browse node. A listing in the wrong place never recovers.
    2. Copy. Title, bullets and description against the category style guide, with search terms covered once and not stuffed.
    3. Images. Main image rules, count, angles, and whether the set answers the buyer's first three questions.
    4. Structure. Parent and child variations, size and colour attributes, and duplicates that split reviews and traffic.
    5. Compliance. Suppressions, restricted claims, missing mandatory attributes. Fix the cause, then log it in the SOP.

    GMV diagnosis tree

    Work out where the revenue went before touching anything

    GMV = visits × conversion rate × average order value

    1. Visits fell? Check search visibility, ad coverage, suppressions and category changes.
    2. Conversion fell? Check content, images, reviews, delivery promise and price against competing offers.
    3. Order value fell? Check discount depth, mix shift and bundle or pack-size availability.
    4. Visits fine, sales still flat? Check stock, Buy Box ownership and the offer that actually shows to the buyer.

    The SOP template

    So the work survives a handover
    1. Purpose and owner. One sentence on why the process exists and one named person accountable for it.
    2. Trigger and inputs. What starts it, and what must be in hand before step one.
    3. Steps with screenshots. Written so someone new can run it without asking.
    4. Quality check. A short checklist that decides pass or rework.
    5. Escalation and revision log. Who to call when it breaks, and what changed since the last version.
